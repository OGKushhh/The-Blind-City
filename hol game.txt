# THE LAST BLOCK — COMPLETE DESIGN WORKLOG (GDD)

## 1. THE CORE LOOP (The "Daily" Ritual)

1. **Hub Phase (Apartment)**: 
   - Listen to the **Radio** (1 Rumor per run).
   - Load/Harvest **The Marrow Still** (distill monster bones & blood into debuff ammo).
   - Craft gear & manage vital supplies at Workbench (Food, Water, Noise Grenades, Medkits).
   - Accept **Survivor Quests** (optional story objectives).
2. **Descent**: Take the elevator to any **unlocked district** (Industrial, Hospital, Police HQ, Subway).
3. **Exploration**: Scavenge for vital supplies (food, water, materials, ammo) and story clues. Avoid or fight monsters.
4. **Hibernation Cycle**: The global clock progresses (*The Silence* → *The Whispers* → *The Frenzy*). Monsters escalate in speed, aggression, and hearing radius.
5. **Extraction**: Reach any elevator. Press the button. Survive the **15-second siege** (monsters rush your position).
6. **Relief/Death**: 
   - *Extract*: Keep all gear and scavenged food/water. Run ends.
   - *Die*: Keep equipped weapons. Lose all raw materials, food, & consumables.
7. **Meta-Progression**: Spend resources on permanent upgrades (speed, HP, carry capacity, flashlight battery).

## 2. THE PERSISTENT CITY (Map Design)

- **Layout**: **Fully fixed**. The city is hand-crafted or procedurally generated *once* per save file. Streets, buildings, shortcuts, and elevator locations are **static**.
- **Respawns (Each New Run)**: 
  - Loot containers **respawn** with randomized contents (based on district tables).
  - Monsters **respawn** at randomized positions and patrol routes.
  - Breakable obstacles (barricades, locked doors) reset.
- **Persistent Elements (Never Reset)**:
  - Story keys, survivor quest progress, lore notes.
  - Permanent player upgrades (bought with resources).
  - Apartment decorations/upgrades.
  - Radio Rumor is **new each run**.

**Districts (Launch)**:
| District | Loot Focus | Difficulty | Vibe |
|----------|------------|------------|------|
| **Industrial** | Crafting materials, scrap metal, noise grenade components | Medium | Wide open spaces, few hiding spots |
| **Hospital** | Medkits, bandages, syringes, rare stimulants, sterile water | Medium-High | Tight corridors, many rooms, high monster density |
| **Police HQ** | Ammo, guns, armor, tactical gear | High | Open lobby, armory behind locked doors |
| **Subway** | Electronics, non-perishable food, flashlight batteries | Low-Medium | Narrow tunnels, pitch-black sections |

## 3. THE HIBERNATION CYCLE (The Clock)

No timers, no UI meters. The cycle progresses **globally in real-time** during a run. **Loot is NOT affected by time**—only location.

| Phase | Duration (Approx) | Monster Behavior | Visual/Audio Cue |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|------------------|
| **1: The Silence** | 0–5 min | Monsters are **static statues**. Zero patrols. Hearing radius: tiny (5 tiles). | Complete eerie quiet. Subtle wind, ambient city hum. |
| **2: The Whispers** | 5–12 min | Monsters **awaken**. They wander randomly (procedural paths, no static waypoints needed). Hearing radius doubles (10 tiles). | Low guttural hums, flickering lights. UI icon shifts to "Alert". |
| **3: The Frenzy** | 12+ min | Monsters sprint. Hearing radius triples (15 tiles). They actively hunt toward any sound. Spawn rates increase. | Screeching sirens, howling echoes, red screen vignette. |

**Phase Transition**: Audio cues play globally (e.g., a distant whisper/guttural growl for Phase 2, a siren for Phase 3) so players *feel* the shift without looking at a UI.

## 4. THE EXTRACTION SIEGE (V1 + Future)

**V1 (Launch)**:
- Player interacts with any elevator.
- A **15-second countdown** begins (UI: "ELEVATOR ARRIVING: 15s").
- A loud mechanical **alarm** blares, attracting all monsters within **30 tiles** to rush the elevator.
- Monsters spawned during the siege also join the hunt.
- When countdown hits 0, doors open. Player walks in. Instant extraction. Run ends.

**Future Innovations (Modular)**:

| Innovation | Description | Why It's Great |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Noise Grenade** (Craftable) | Throw a device that emits a loud sound elsewhere. Monsters hard-pivot toward it for 5 seconds. | Tactical diversion during siege or to bypass patrols. |
| **Co-op Sacrifice** | Elevator doors have a pressure plate. If one player stands inside, doors try to close **immediately**. The other can "Hold Doors" (extends timer by 5s) but is vulnerable. | Creates moral choices: "Leave your friend or die together?" |
| **Breach Threat** (Optional) | Monsters attack elevator doors. Doors have 100 HP. If HP hits 0, elevator aborts. You must find another extraction point. | Turns siege into "Protect the Objective." |

## 5. MONSTERS (The Shivers)

> *NOTE: Monster types, behaviors, and names listed below are draft placeholders and subject to change during playtesting.*

- **Blind**: Monsters cannot see. They rely entirely on **hearing**.
- **No "Partial Sight"**: They stay blind forever.
- **Static-to-Active**: Phase 1 = statues. Phase 2 = random wander. Phase 3 = sprint.

**Behavior by Phase**:

| Phase | Behavior |
|-------|----------|
| **Phase 1: The Silence** | Standing still. No reaction to footsteps. Reaction to gunshots only (brief investigation, then reset). |
| **Phase 2: The Whispers** | Wander randomly (procedural movement, picking random nearby points). React to footsteps (investigate). React to gunshots (rush). |
| **Phase 3: The Frenzy** | Sprint. React to footsteps (rush instantly). React to gunshots (swarm with extra speed). |

**Draft Monster Types (V1 Base)**:
- **Rusher**: Fast, low HP. Standard enemy.
- **Brute**: Slow, high HP, deals massive damage.
- **Screamer**: Fragile. When alerted, it shrieks, pulling all monsters in a 50-tile radius to its position.

## 6. LOOT SYSTEM

- **Location-Based Tables**: Each district has a fixed loot table.
- **Randomness**: Within the table, rarity is random (Common / Uncommon / Rare).
- **Time does NOT affect loot**. Phase 3 doesn't give better loot—it just makes reaching the loot more dangerous.

**Example Loot Tables**:
| District | Common (60%) | Uncommon (30%) | Rare (10%) |
|----------|--------------|----------------|------------|
| **Hospital** | Bandage, Bottled Water | Medkit, Syringe | Stimulant (temp speed boost) |
| **Police HQ** | Pistol Ammo | Shotgun Ammo | Tier 2 Armor |
| **Industrial** | Scrap Metal, Canned Food | Noise Grenade Parts | Rare Crafting Component |
| **Subway** | Flashlight Battery, Clean Water | Electronics | Lore Fragment (story item) |

## 7. DEATH & META-PROGRESSION

**Death Penalty**:
- You **keep** equipped weapons (guns, melee).
- You **lose** all raw materials, consumables, food, water, monster parts, and uninsured loot in your backpack.
- Story keys, lore notes, and quest items are **persistent** (you keep them).

**Meta-Progression (Roguelike)**:
After death or extraction, spend resources (scrap, blood, electronics) on permanent upgrades:
- **Movement Speed** (+5% per level, max 5 levels).
- **Max Health** (+10 HP per level, max 5 levels).
- **Carrying Capacity** (+2 slots per level, max 5 levels).
- **Flashlight Battery** (+15% duration per level, max 5 levels).
- **Stamina/Noise Reduction** (reduce footstep noise by 10% per level).

## 8. THE APARTMENT HUB (The Safe Zone)

- **Storage Locker**: Store weapons, food, water, meds, and rare items between runs (shared across co-op).
- **Crafting Bench**: 
  - Noise Grenade = Scrap + Electronics.
  - Medkit = 2x Bandages + 1x Syringe.
  - Ammo = Scrap + Gunpowder.
  - Clean Water = Unfiltered Water + Syringe/Filter.
- **The Marrow Still & Bone Press**: 
  - Combine **Bullet Casings** + **Monster Blood** (propellant) + **Monster Bones/Organs** (debuff properties).
  - Takes **1 completed run** to distill and press into a special magazine.
  - *Yields tactical debuff rounds*:
    - **Brute Bone** → *Crushing Shard Rounds* (Slows monster movement by 50%).
    - **Screamer Tissue** → *Deafening Slugs* (Deafens target so it can't hear gunshots/footsteps for 6s).
    - **Rusher Marrow** → *Corrosive Needle Rounds* (Melts armor / Damage over time).
    - **Phosphor Tissue** → *Tracer Slugs* (Highlights enemies through walls in darkness).
- **The Radio (CRITICAL)**: 
  - Plays **one random Rumor** per run *before* you descend.
  - If you leave without listening, you play a **"Neutral" run** (no modifiers).
  - The Rumor **overrides** the rules for a specific district for that run only.

## 9. CO-OP (2-4 Players)

- **Shared Screen**: Camera centers on all players. If they split far apart, camera zooms out (monsters get a slight "alert" buff when players split beyond 20 tiles).
- **Revive**: Downed player enters a **bleed-out state** (30 seconds). Teammate presses 'E' to revive (takes 3s, immobilizes reviver). Alternatively, use a Medkit for instant revive.
- **Sacrifice Mechanic**: During extraction siege, only one player needs to be inside the elevator for departure. Others can "Hold Doors" (hold E) to extend timer by 5s, but cannot shoot while holding.
- **Balance**: Monster spawns and HP scale with player count (2x HP for 2 players, 3x for 3, etc.).
