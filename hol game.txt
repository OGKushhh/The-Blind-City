# THE BLIND CITY — COMPLETE DESIGN WORKLOG (GDD)
**Version 1.4 | 2D Top-Down Survival-Horror | Single-Player + Co-op (2-4)**  
*Inspired by: Hell is Others, Dying Light, Darkest Dungeon (atmosphere)*

---

## 1. THE CORE LOOP (The "Daily" Ritual)

1. **Hub Phase (Apartment)**:
   - Listen to the **Radio** (1 Rumor per run).
   - Load/Harvest **The Marrow Still** (distill monster bones & blood into debuff ammo).
   - Craft gear & manage vital supplies at Workbench (Food, Water, Noise Grenades, Medkits).
   - Accept **Survivor Quests** (optional story objectives).

2. **Descent**: Take the elevator to any **unlocked district** (Industrial, Hospital, Police HQ, Subway).

3. **Exploration**: Scavenge for vital supplies (food, water, materials, ammo) and story clues. Avoid or fight monsters.

4. **Hibernation Cycle**: The global clock progresses (*The Silence* → *The Whispers* → *The Frenzy*). Monsters escalate in speed, aggression, and hearing radius.

5. **Extraction**: Reach any elevator. Press the button. Survive the **15-second siege** (monsters rush your position).

6. **Relief/Death**:
   - *Extract*: Keep all gear and scavenged food/water. Run ends.
   - *Die*: Keep equipped weapons. Lose all raw materials, food, & consumables.

7. **Meta-Progression**: Spend resources on permanent upgrades (speed, HP, carry capacity, flashlight battery).

## 2. THE PERSISTENT CITY (Map Design)

- **Layout**: **Fully fixed**. The city is hand-crafted or procedurally generated *once* per save file. Streets, buildings, shortcuts, and elevator locations are **static**.
- **Respawns (Each New Run)**:
  - Loot containers **respawn** with randomized contents (based on district tables).
  - Monsters **respawn** at randomized positions and patrol routes.
  - Breakable obstacles (barricades, locked doors) reset.
- **Persistent Elements (Never Reset)**:
  - Story keys, survivor quest progress, lore notes.
  - Permanent player upgrades (bought with resources).
  - Apartment decorations/upgrades.
  - Radio Rumor is **new each run**.

**Districts (Launch)**:

| District | Loot Focus | Difficulty | Vibe |
|----------|------------|------------|------|
| **Industrial** | Crafting materials, scrap metal, noise grenade components | Medium | Wide open spaces, few hiding spots |
| **Hospital** | Medkits, bandages, syringes, rare stimulants, sterile water | Medium-High | Tight corridors, many rooms, high monster density |
| **Police HQ** | Ammo, guns, armor, tactical gear | High | Open lobby, armory behind locked doors |
| **Subway** | Electronics, non-perishable food, flashlight batteries | Low-Medium | Narrow tunnels, pitch-black sections |

## 3. THE HIBERNATION CYCLE (The Clock)

No timers, no UI meters. The cycle progresses **globally in real-time** during a run. **Loot is NOT affected by time**—only location.

| Phase | Duration (Approx) | Monster Behavior | Visual/Audio Cue |
|-------|-------------------|------------------|------------------|
| **1: The Silence** | 0–5 min | Monsters are **static statues**. No reaction to footsteps. **Investigate gunshots** (walk to location, search 3-5 seconds, return to static if player not found). | Complete eerie quiet. Subtle wind, ambient city hum. |
| **2: The Whispers** | 5–12 min | Monsters **awaken**. They wander randomly (procedural paths, no static waypoints needed). Hearing radius doubles (10 tiles). | Low guttural hums, flickering lights. UI icon shifts to "Alert". |
| **3: The Frenzy** | 12+ min | Monsters sprint. Hearing radius triples (15 tiles). They actively hunt toward any sound. | Screeching sirens, howling echoes, red screen vignette. |

**Phase Transition**: Audio cues play globally (e.g., a distant whisper/guttural growl for Phase 2, a siren for Phase 3) so players *feel* the shift without looking at a UI.

## 4. THE EXTRACTION SIEGE (V1 + Future)

**V1 (Launch)**:
- Player interacts with any elevator.
- A **15-second countdown** begins (UI: "ELEVATOR ARRIVING: 15s").
- A loud mechanical **alarm** blares, attracting all monsters within **30 tiles** to rush the elevator.
- Only **existing** monsters rush the elevator. **No new monsters spawn** during the siege.
- When countdown hits 0, doors open. Player walks in. Instant extraction. Run ends.

**Future Innovations (Modular)**:

| Innovation | Description | Why It's Great |
|------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Noise Grenade** (Craftable) | Throw a device that emits a loud sound elsewhere. Monsters hard-pivot toward it for 5 seconds. | Tactical diversion during siege or to bypass patrols. |
| **Co-op Sacrifice** | Elevator doors have a pressure plate. If one player stands inside, doors try to close **immediately**. The other can "Hold Doors" (extends timer by 5s) but is vulnerable. | Creates moral choices: "Leave your friend or die together?" |
| **Breach Threat** (Optional) | Monsters attack elevator doors. Doors have 100 HP. If HP hits 0, elevator aborts. You must find another extraction point. | Turns siege into "Protect the Objective." |

## 5. MONSTERS (The Shivers & Remnants)

> *NOTE: Monster types, behaviors, and names listed below are draft placeholders and subject to change during playtesting.*

### Tier 1: The Shivers (Hearing-Based)

All Shivers are **blind**. They rely entirely on **hearing**. No "partial sight." They stay blind forever.

**Behavior by Phase**:

| Phase | Rusher | Brute | Lurker |
|-------|--------|-------|--------|
| **Phase 1: The Silence** | Static statue. No reaction to footsteps. **Investigates gunshots** (walks to sound location, searches 3-5 seconds, returns to original static position if player not found). | Static statue. No reaction to footsteps. **Investigates gunshots** (walks to sound location, searches 3-5 seconds, returns to original static position if player not found). | Static statue. No reaction. (No hearing.) |
| **Phase 2: The Whispers** | Random roam. Reacts to footsteps (investigate). Reacts to gunshots (rush to location). | Random roam. Reacts to gunshots only (rush to location). Ignores footsteps. | Moves outside 120° view cone. Freezes inside cone. |
| **Phase 3: The Frenzy** | Sprints toward any sound. | Sprints toward gunshots only. Ignores footsteps. | Moves faster outside cone. Freezes inside cone. |

**Investigation Behavior (Phase 1 - Gunshots)**:
- On gunshot, the monster wakes from its static state.
- It moves toward the gunshot location at **walking speed**.
- Upon reaching the location, it **searches** for 3-5 seconds (small random patrol around the area).
- If it doesn't detect the player (via sound or contact), it returns to its **original static position** and resets to statue state.
- If it *does* detect the player (footsteps, or the player shoots again), it transitions to **Phase 2 behavior** (chase/investigate) permanently for that monster (it stays awake).

**Lurker Detailed Behavior**:
- **View Cone**: 120° centered on player's aim direction.
- **Movement**: Moves at walking speed (Phase 2) or sprint speed (Phase 3) ONLY when outside the cone.
- **Freeze**: Instantly stops when the cone overlaps with it.
- **Attack**: If it reaches melee range (2 tiles) while player isn't looking, it attacks immediately.
- **Counter-Play**: Sweep your aim to "check" corners and doorways.

---

### Tier 1.5: The Remnants (Trap Monsters - No Hearing)

These do NOT react to sound. They are environmental hazards.

| Name | Classification | Behavior | Phase Restriction |
|------|---------------|----------|-------------------|
| **The Effigy** | Statue Trap | Looks like decorative statue (mannequin, gargoyle, angel). Moves only when player's **back is turned** (180° behind). Freezes when player faces it. Moves silently. Attacks when reaching melee range. | Active in **all phases**. |
| **The Hanger** | Wall Trap | Clings to ceilings/walls. Lunges/drops when player walks directly beneath it (within 2 tiles). Deals damage and briefly slows the player. | Active only in **Phase 2+** (sleeps in Phase 1). |

**Counter-Play**:
- **Effigy**: Look behind you when entering new rooms. Stare at it while backing away.
- **Hanger**: Look up! Shoot before passing under, or take a wide berth.

**Key Distinction**: The Lurker (Shivers) is tied to your **view cone** (120° forward). The Effigy (Remnants) is tied to your **back** (global 180° behind). This creates two distinct stealth threats: one that punishes tunnel vision, one that punishes carelessness.

## 6. MONSTER SPAWNING RULES (Simplified)

| Rule | Decision |
|------|----------|
| **Spawn Timing** | All monsters (Shivers, Remnants, Apex) are placed on the map **at run start**. |
| **Dynamic Spawns** | **None.** No vents, no closets, no siege spawns. |
| **Extraction Siege** | Only **existing** monsters rush the elevator. **No new spawns.** |
| **Death** | Monsters are **permanently removed** when killed. **No respawns.** |
| **Clearing** | If you kill all monsters in a district, that district becomes **completely safe** for the rest of the run. |

## 7. LOOT SYSTEM

- **Location-Based Tables**: Each district has a fixed loot table.
- **Randomness**: Within the table, rarity is random (Common / Uncommon / Rare).
- **Time does NOT affect loot**. Phase 3 doesn't give better loot—it just makes reaching the loot more dangerous.

**Example Loot Tables**:

| District | Common (60%) | Uncommon (30%) | Rare (10%) |
|----------|--------------|----------------|------------|
| **Hospital** | Bandage, Bottled Water | Medkit, Syringe | Stimulant (temp speed boost) |
| **Police HQ** | Pistol Ammo | Shotgun Ammo | Tier 2 Armor |
| **Industrial** | Scrap Metal, Canned Food | Noise Grenade Parts | Rare Crafting Component |
| **Subway** | Flashlight Battery, Clean Water | Electronics | Lore Fragment (story item) |

## 8. DEATH & META-PROGRESSION

**Death Penalty**:
- You **keep** equipped weapons (guns, melee).
- You **lose** all raw materials, consumables, food, water, monster parts, and uninsured loot in your backpack.
- Story keys, lore notes, and quest items are **persistent** (you keep them).

**Meta-Progression (Roguelike)**:
After death or extraction, spend resources (scrap, blood, electronics) on permanent upgrades:
- **Movement Speed** (+5% per level, max 5 levels).
- **Max Health** (+10 HP per level, max 5 levels).
- **Carrying Capacity** (+2 slots per level, max 5 levels).
- **Flashlight Battery** (+15% duration per level, max 5 levels).
- **Stamina/Noise Reduction** (reduce footstep noise by 10% per level).

## 9. THE APARTMENT HUB (The Safe Zone)

- **Storage Locker**: Store weapons, food, water, meds, and rare items between runs (shared across co-op).
- **Crafting Bench**:
  - Noise Grenade = Scrap + Electronics.
  - Medkit = 2x Bandages + 1x Syringe.
  - Ammo = Scrap + Gunpowder.
  - Clean Water = Unfiltered Water + Syringe/Filter.
- **The Marrow Still & Bone Press**:
  - Combine **Bullet Casings** + **Monster Blood** (propellant) + **Monster Bones/Organs** (debuff properties).
  - Takes **1 completed run** to distill and press into a special magazine.
  - *Yields tactical debuff rounds*:

| Ammo Type | Ingredients | Crafting Time | Effect |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| Crushing Shard Rounds | Bullet Casing + Blood + Brute Bone | 1 Run | Slows monsters by 50% for 3s |
| Deafening Slugs | Bullet Casing + Blood + Screamer Tissue | 1 Run | Deafens target (can't hear) for 6s |
| Corrosive Needle Rounds | Bullet Casing + Blood + Rusher Marrow | 1 Run | Damage over time (5 DPS for 5s) |
| Tracer Slugs | Bullet Casing + Blood + Phosphor Tissue | 1 Run | Highlights enemies through darkness |

- **The Radio (CRITICAL)**:
  - Plays **one random Rumor** per run *before* you descend.
  - If you leave without listening, you play a **"Neutral" run** (no modifiers).
  - The Rumor **overrides** the rules for a specific district for that run only.
  - All other districts remain accessible in their neutral state.

## 10. THE RADIO RUMOR SYSTEM

| Rumor Name | Broadcast Text | Gameplay Override | Strategic Impact |
|------------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **"Empty Nest"** | *"Heard the Shivers are migrating to the Subway today. Hospital is dead quiet."* | **Hospital District**: 50% fewer monsters. Loot is standard (no bonus). | **Safe looting.** Ideal for beginners or low-health runs. |
| **"Golden Haul"** | *"A supply chopper crashed in the Industrial Zone. Heard it's loaded."* | **Industrial District**: Spawns **1 Guaranteed Rare Crate** (endgame gun OR 3x medkits) in a fixed landmark. All other loot in that district is disabled. | **High-risk target.** You know exactly where the treasure is, but monsters cluster around it. |
| **"Blackout"** | *"Power grid is failing in the Subway. It's pitch black down there."* | **Subway District**: No ambient streetlights. Only your flashlight works. Monsters start in **Phase 2** (The Whispers) immediately. | **Atmospheric terror.** Forces flashlight management and tight corridor fights. |
| **"Horde Migration"** | *"The tunnels are flooded. They're all moving to the surface."* | **All Districts**: +25% more monster spawns. BUT extraction elevators arrive in **5 seconds** (instead of 15). | **Chaos vs. Safety.** More enemies, but a faster escape. Great for aggressive players. |
| **"Survivor Signal"** | *"A survivor is broadcasting from the Police HQ. They have medicine."* | **Police HQ**: Spawns a **friendly NPC survivor** in a locked room. If you reach them, they give you a **Lore Fragment** (story progression) and a permanent buff (e.g., +10 max HP). | **Story progression.** Essential for 100% completion. |

**Design Note**: The Rumor **overrides** the district's standard rules—it does not add on top. This forces a sharp choice: "Do I follow the rumor for a specific reward, or ignore it and play a neutral, predictable run?"

## 11. CO-OP (2-4 Players)

- **Shared Screen**: Camera centers on all players. If they split far apart, camera zooms out (monsters get a slight "alert" buff when players are separated beyond 20 tiles).
- **Revive**: Downed player enters a **bleed-out state** (30 seconds). Teammate can press 'E' to revive (takes 3 seconds, immobilizes the reviver). Alternatively, use a rare **Medkit** for instant revive.
- **Sacrifice Mechanic**: During extraction siege, only one player needs to be inside the elevator for it to depart. Other players can "Hold Doors" (press and hold E) to extend the timer by 5 seconds, but they are vulnerable and cannot shoot while holding.
- **Balance**: Monster spawns and HP scale with player count (2x HP for 2 players, 3x for 3, etc.).

## 12. LORE SETUP & WIN CONDITION

**Lore Setup**:
- **2 weeks ago**, the sun suddenly vanished. Eternal night fell over the city.
- In the immediate chaos and panic, "The Shivers" emerged—blind, mutated horrors that react entirely to sound. Society collapsed almost overnight.
- You are an apartment survivor facing the fresh panic of this collapse. Your goal: descend into the city to scavenge food, water, and vital supplies, piece together story clues about what happened, and survive.
- The city was officially named something else before the collapse. Survivors now grimly joke that it's **"Happy City"** —a sarcastic name for a place that's anything but happy.

**Win Condition / Endgame Goal**:
> *[STATUS: DRAFT / PLACEHOLDER - Subject to redesign]*
- Find **7 Lore Fragments** scattered across high-risk districts and rumors.
- Unlocks access to the final area (**The Underground Lab**).
- Survive the Lab and extract to trigger the ending credits.
